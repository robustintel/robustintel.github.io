02 / Capabilities

Six pillars of robust intelligence.

01

Trustworthy AI by construction.

Reliability, robustness, and verifiability built into the model, the software, and the silicon. Systems that behave predictably under noise, drift, and adversarial input.

Robust AI Reliable Systems Verifiability
02

Edge AI for milliwatt budgets.

Energy-efficient computing across the stack: model compression, custom accelerators, and runtime systems that put intelligence where the energy is scarce.

Edge AI Low-Power On-Device
03

Sensor-to-decision processing.

Pipelines that compress, denoise, and reason at the source. Multimodal fusion of vision, audio, RF, and physical signals, with the bandwidth, latency, and power of the place they actually run.

Multimodal AI Intelligent Sensing Fusion
04

Autonomy under uncertainty.

Closed-loop perception, prediction, and control for vehicles, drones, and platforms operating in unknown or contested environments where ground truth is partial.

Autonomous Systems Decision AI Closed-loop
05

Anomaly & event intelligence.

Real-time anomaly detection, predictive monitoring, and fault diagnosis on streaming sensor data, designed to flag the rare event before it becomes a failure.

Anomaly Detection Predictive Monitoring Real-Time
06

Distributed & space-grade AI.

Intelligence that survives where the network does not: distributed sensing networks, wearable devices, and AI built for the radiation, thermal, and bandwidth realities of orbit.

Distributed Sensing Wearable Space Systems AI
